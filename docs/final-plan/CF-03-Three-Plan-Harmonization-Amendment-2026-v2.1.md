## Historical CF03-FR-043

Eligible CF-03 financial assets were owner-scoped invoice snapshots, owner-scoped donation/payment receipts, requester/authorized-finance scoped secure export artifacts and verified aggregate public transparency snapshots. CF-03 determined eligibility and current authorization while File 20 could manage global download progress and File 25 presentation.

A grant included a stable asset reference, safe filename, approved media type, SHA-256 checksum, audience, policy version, creation/expiry timestamps and an approved opaque or vault-scoped delivery reference. Restricted, expired, revoked, not-ready, privacy-restricted or unauthorized assets were to be hidden or denied without exposing a delivery reference.
