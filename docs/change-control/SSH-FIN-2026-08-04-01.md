## Governing decision

Sabri Social Homeopathy Platform shall remain completely free for all users until the complete platform design, implementation, staging acceptance and Founder review are concluded.

All previously planned membership, education, AI or platform service charges shall remain dormant and non-collectible during this period. They are retained only as historical/versioned product and price definitions and cannot be used to create a checkout, subscription, invoice or entitlement-changing financial fact.

A voluntary donation appeal may be shown to each user no more than once in seven days. The permitted suggested amounts are USD 10, USD 14, USD 50 and a positive custom USD amount. Monthly recurring support is optional, requires explicit consent and is never preselected.

Donations shall never influence access, ranking, verification, moderation, visibility, support priority, clinic eligibility, marketplace eligibility, education, clinical access or any essential service.

## Free-service boundary

The following are currently free and must not create a charge or payment method requirement:

- registration and general membership;
- public knowledge and reading;
- basic education access;
- ordinary doctor, patient and member platform features;
- all core platform services;
- Clinic and Marketplace platform commission, which remains 0%.

Prohibited while this decision is active:

- paid membership or education checkout;
- AI add-on or usage charge;
- platform service fee, hidden fee or automatic billing;
- trial-expiry conversion into payment;
- card requirement for free access;
- silent renewal or preselected recurring consent.

## Donation appeal contract

### Suggested choices

- USD 10
- USD 14
- USD 50
- Custom Amount

No amount is selected by default. `Make this a monthly donation` is unchecked by default.

Actions:

- Support Now
- Remind Me Later
- Not Now
- Close

The user must be able to dismiss the appeal without friction, guilt language, blocked reading or degraded service.

## Frequency and suppression law

Logged-in server-side fields:

- `last_donation_prompt_at`
- `donation_prompt_status`
- `donation_prompt_snoozed_until`
- `last_donation_completed_at`
- `donation_frequency_preference`

Guest first-party keys:

- `sabri_donation_prompt_seen`
- `sabri_donation_prompt_next_at`

Rules:

1. The appeal cannot appear again before seven days after it was shown.
2. Remind Me Later, Not Now and Close each create a minimum seven-day snooze.
3. A completed one-time donation suppresses the appeal for at least thirty days.
4. An active monthly donor does not receive the general donation appeal.
5. A checkout failure suppresses further appeal popups in the same session.
6. The appeal appears at most once per page view.
7. It requires at least thirty seconds of use or a meaningful interaction/scroll.
8. It is prohibited during login, registration, password recovery, guardian consent, clinical consultation, emergency warning, support appeal and payment-error contexts.
