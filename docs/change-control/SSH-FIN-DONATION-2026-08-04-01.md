The historical model used suggested amounts of USD 10, USD 14, USD 50 and positive custom USD. No amount or monthly recurrence was preselected. Actions were Donate, Remind Me Later, Not Now and Close.

The historical appeal appeared at most once per calendar month. Remind Me Later, Not Now, Close and a completed donation suppressed it for at least 30 days. Active monthly donors received no general appeal. These frequency/recurrence provisions are superseded and must not be reactivated by current code.
