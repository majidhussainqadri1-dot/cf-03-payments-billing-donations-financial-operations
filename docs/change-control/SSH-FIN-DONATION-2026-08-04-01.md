## Final decision

Sabri Social Homeopathy Platform currently operates without a fixed membership, education, AI, registration, profile, verification, listing, publishing, access or other core-platform fee. Clinic and Marketplace commission is 0%. Financial support is requested only as a voluntary one-time or monthly donation.

Donation never affects access, membership, verification, ranking, profile, treatment, publishing, moderation, support, clinic, marketplace, education, search, AI priority or any core service.

## Ownership and non-Trust status

The platform, domain, code, repositories, architecture, design, databases, content and intellectual property remain the property of Dr. Allamah Majid Hussain Sabri Muhaddith Murshid. A donation creates no ownership, partnership, shareholding, governance, voting, intellectual-property, management or mandatory profit right.

The platform is not a Trust, charitable trust, welfare trust or trust fund, shall not be registered as one, and shall be described as a Founder-Owned Platform supported through voluntary donations.

## Approved uses

Donations may be used only for institutional survival and operations, hosting/servers/security/backups, development/testing/maintenance, communication/payment-provider costs, lawful legal/accounting/administration, staff and office resources, resilience, approved Founder compensation or expense repayment, platform development, and homeopathy education, research, translation, books, courses, archives and national/global advancement.

Unrelated charity, grants, political/election activity, unrelated investment, personal luxury and donor privilege are prohibited without a new Founder-approved Change-Control.

## Donation Appeal

Suggested amounts are USD 10, USD 14, USD 50 and positive custom USD. No amount or monthly recurrence is preselected. Actions are Donate, Remind Me Later, Not Now and Close.

The appeal may appear at most once per calendar month. Remind Me Later, Not Now, Close and a completed donation suppress it for at least 30 days. Active monthly donors receive no general appeal. Repeated page/session prompts, payment-failure loops and sensitive authentication/clinical/support contexts are prohibited.

Logged-in fields are `last_donation_prompt_at`, `next_donation_prompt_at`, `donation_prompt_status`, `donation_prompt_snoozed_until`, `last_donation_completed_at` and `recurring_donation_status`. Guest keys are `sabri_donation_prompt_seen_at` and `sabri_donation_prompt_next_at`.

## Financial transparency

A public `/transparency/` page publishes verified aggregate totals for lifetime/month/year donations, balance, total expenses, institutional operations, technical development, homeopathy advancement, administration/payment charges and the separate Founder categories: compensation, expense reimbursement, advance repayment and owner withdrawal.

Donor identity, bank/card details, payment credentials, security-sensitive vendor information and private invoices remain protected. Anonymous donation is available. Public acknowledgment requires explicit revocable consent and creates no privilege.

## Payment security and management

Only hosted or tokenized providers may be used. PAN, CVV, PIN, OTP, banking passwords and raw payment credentials shall not be accepted, stored or logged. Webhooks must be signed, replay-protected, idempotent, provider-verified and audited. Monthly donors receive clear view, next-payment-date, supported amount-change, cancellation, receipt and support contracts; cancellation must not be deliberately difficult.

## Change-Control lock

No fixed-fee rule, Founder ownership, non-Trust status, voluntary donation, approved uses, transparency, Founder disclosure, donation/non-privilege separation, monthly frequency cap or credential-security rule may change without a new written Founder approval documenting old/new rule, reasons, affected files, financial/security/privacy effects, migrations, tests and rollback.
